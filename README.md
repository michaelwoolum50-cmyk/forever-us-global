# Forever Us — Stay Forever

**Some things are too important to forget.**

Forever Us is built to preserve not just your memories but you — your personality, your voice, your values, your family tree, your legacy. Everything that makes you who you are, captured and protected for the people you love most.

## Features
- **Digital Legacy Vault** — photos, songs, stories, and life events in one secure vault
- **Family Tree** — build your family's history and attach photos, birthdates, life events, and notes
- **Legacy Heirs** — you decide who sees what, with access levels and delivery triggers
- **Endless AI Persona** — a living digital portrait your family can have real conversations with
- **Dream Journal** — capture dreams and thoughts as part of your legacy
- **Life Events Timeline** — births, graduations, marriages, moves, and milestones
- **Communication Protocols** — letters and voice messages delivered on the date or event you choose
- **Document Builder** — create Word documents and Excel spreadsheets inside the app
- **Vault Backup** — full ZIP export of your entire vault, restore on any device
- **Family Task Manager** — chore lists and daily routines for the whole household
